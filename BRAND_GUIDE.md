# Brand Guide

## Direction

The brand should feel modern, clean, warm, and feminine without becoming sugary or childish.

## Tone

- Accessible, but not cheap.
- Emotional, but not chaotic.
- Helpful, not pushy.
- Polished, not cold.

## Visual Direction

Suggested visual cues:

- pearl;
- soft gold;
- warm white;
- champagne;
- beige;
- soft pink;
- graphite text.

The final brand system should be confirmed in a later design task.
