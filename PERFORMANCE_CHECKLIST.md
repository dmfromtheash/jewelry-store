# Performance Checklist

## Core Web Vitals Goals

- Keep LCP fast with optimized product images.
- Avoid layout shift in product cards and galleries.
- Keep interactions responsive.

## Practical Rules

- Optimize and size images.
- Lazy-load non-critical images.
- Avoid heavy sliders and background videos.
- Avoid unnecessary dependencies.
- Keep scripts small and purposeful.
- Test mobile performance before launch.
