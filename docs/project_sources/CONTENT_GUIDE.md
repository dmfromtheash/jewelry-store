# Content Guide

## Product Names

Use clear names that combine product type, material or finish, and a memorable detail.

Example pattern:

`Product type + key visual detail + finish or material`

## Short Descriptions

Keep short descriptions specific and useful:

- mention the style;
- mention the main material or finish;
- mention the occasion or pairing when relevant;
- avoid exaggerated claims.

## PDP Description Structure

Use this structure for product detail pages:

- short emotional intro;
- material and finish;
- size, length, or fit details;
- styling suggestions;
- care notes;
- delivery and return notes.

## FAQ Topics

- materials and coating;
- size guide;
- delivery;
- returns;
- care;
- gift packaging.

## Forbidden Content

- fake promises;
- medical claims;
- aggressive manipulation;
- fake scarcity;
- unsupported luxury claims.
