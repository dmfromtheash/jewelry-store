# E-commerce Checklist

## Catalog

- Categories are clear.
- Product cards show image, name, price, stock state, and quick action.
- Filters and sorting are understandable.
- Empty states are useful.

## PDP

- Product images are visible and optimized.
- Price, variants, stock, delivery, return, and care details are clear.
- Add to cart and wishlist states are explicit.

## Cart

- Items can be reviewed and changed.
- Totals are clear.
- Empty cart state suggests a next step.

## Checkout

- Demo checkout does not process real payments.
- Validation is clear.
- Success state is visible.

## Trust

- Delivery, return, care, and contact information are easy to find.

## Admin Later

- Admin tooling is out of scope until a separate task.
